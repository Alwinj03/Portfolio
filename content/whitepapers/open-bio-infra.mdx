## Abstract

Biological research is bottlenecked less by ideas than by infrastructure. This
paper argues for a commons of open, composable tools and datasets, and outlines
the principles such infrastructure should follow.

## Thesis

1. Reproducibility is a first-class design goal.
2. Interfaces matter more than implementations.
3. Openness compounds.
