Won **First Prize** at the BioIdeathon (International Conference on Bioengineering, SRMIST × Boltzmann Labs) — went from **80 teams → top 15 → 1st place**.

**The problem:** acrylamide, a possible carcinogen that forms when food hits high heat.

**Our move:** a computational strategy to reduce it, combining —

- Reaction pathway analysis
- Molecular interaction site ID
- AI-assisted molecular generation
- RDKit cheminformatics
- Descriptor + molecular filtering
- Retrosynthetic feasibility checks

Proof that **AI-guided molecular design** can prototype real food-safety fixes fast.
