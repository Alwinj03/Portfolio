got selected for the **INSA–IASC–NASI summer research fellowship** — one of india's most prestigious undergrad research programs, run jointly by the country's three national science academies (legacy traces back to **c.v. raman**).

did my research at **iit kharagpur** in the **systems & synthetic biology lab** under **dr. amit ghosh**.

worked on the **Community-wide Microbial Interaction Network (CMIN)** — digging into microbial metabolism and computational ways to understand the communities behind biomass degradation and sustainable bioprocessing.
