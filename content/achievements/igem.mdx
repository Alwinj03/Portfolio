Reached the finalist stage of the **iGEM BioHackathon**, advancing from **500+ participants** as an undergraduate member of a multidisciplinary team that included PhD researchers and industry professionals from across the world.

Our team proposed **BioProNN**, a vision for distributed and democratized biomanufacturing inspired by the question:

> **What if biology had its own internet?**

The concept imagined a future where community laboratories, makerspaces, and educational institutions function as interconnected biological nodes, collaboratively running experiments, sharing knowledge, and improving through federated learning.

The experience reinforced a belief that the future of biotechnology will be built not only through better biology, but through better systems, infrastructure, and collaboration.
