I work at the intersection of **artificial intelligence, biotechnology, and
engineering** — building computational tools that help us read, model, and
extend living systems.

My focus right now is **scientific machine learning for biology**: foundation
models for biological data, automation for the wet lab, and the open
infrastructure that lets small teams do frontier science.

I believe biology is the most sophisticated technology ever written, and that
the next decade belongs to the people who can engineer it responsibly — with
elegance, curiosity, and a commitment to open science.
