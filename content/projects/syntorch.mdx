## Overview

Syntorch treats genetic circuit design like a neural network: define
components, connect them, and let gradient-based optimization tune the
parameters toward a target behavior.

## Architecture

- A composable circuit graph
- A differentiable simulator for ODE-based kinetics
- An optimizer that fits designs to specifications
